# Third-Party Assets & Provenance Ledger

Reduced-Town Stage 1 follows a two-tier asset architecture so the project runs immediately with realistic PBR geometry/textures and also seamlessly upgrades to external CC0 packs when fetched via `tools/fetch_cc0_assets.sh`.

## 1. Bundled Project PBR Models, Textures, Audio & Icons (CC0 / Project Original)

| Category | Assets | Source / Generation | License |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 3D Models (glTF 2.0 `.glb`) | `assets/models/bldg_*.glb`, `car_body.glb`, `car_wheel.glb`, `prop_*.glb`, `landmark_clock_fountain.glb` | Procedurally sculpted architectural/vehicle/prop geometry generated by `tools/generate_pbr_and_audio.py` | CC0 1.0 Universal |
| PBR Textures (`.png`) | `assets/textures/*_{albedo,normal,roughness}.png`, `contact_shadow.png` | Procedurally synthesized seamless PBR textures (asphalt aggregate, concrete pavers, red/tan brick courses, stucco plaster, window glazing, bark, foliage, tire tread, radial AO contact shadow) generated by `tools/generate_pbr_and_audio.py` | CC0 1.0 Universal |
| Audio (`.wav`) | `assets/audio/ambient_city_afternoon.wav`, `footstep_asphalt_01..03.wav`, `engine_loop.wav`, `car_door.wav`, `car_bump.wav` | Procedurally synthesized acoustic waveforms (filtered pink-noise breeze & distant city bed, multi-band asphalt shoe strikes, harmonic V6/inline-4 combustion loop, damped door latch thud, low-end chassis impact) generated by `tools/generate_pbr_and_audio.py` | CC0 1.0 Universal |
| UI Vector Icons (`.svg`) | `assets/icons/ic_*.svg` | Original unified-stroke vector line icons authored for Reduced-Town (zero emojis) | CC0 1.0 Universal |

## 2. Optional External CC0 Asset Packs (`tools/fetch_cc0_assets.sh`)

When `tools/fetch_cc0_assets.sh` is executed, `AssetResolver.gd` automatically detects and priority-loads the extracted `.gltf`/`.glb` assets from `assets/cc0/` while preserving all collision shapes, lighting, contact shadows, and per-instance prop variations.
